The problem

One book. Too many readers.

In many primary and high school classrooms, a single course book is shared by a group of pupils. Homework waits for a turn. Revision happens in short borrowed moments. Learning slows down for everyone at the desk.

A book of your own changes that. You can read ahead, revise at home and keep notes in the margins.

Today: 1 book, 3 pupils
Our goal: 1 book each

Where we're heading

From books to bicycles

Now

A course book for every pupil

Our first goal is simple: no more sharing. Each pupil and student in our partner schools gets their own course books.

As we grow

Bicycles for long journeys

Many pupils walk long distances and arrive at school tired before the first lesson. A bicycle cuts that journey short, so they arrive with energy and a clear head, ready to learn.

Measuring impact

We don't hand over books and walk away

We want to see what a book of your own actually does for a pupil. Working with each school, we follow performance before and after resources arrive, and use what we learn to decide where to go next.

Set a baseline

Record each pupil's results before the books arrive.

Follow every term

Track progress term by term with the school's own assessments.

Reward improvement

Award the pupils who improve the most, so progress is noticed and celebrated.

Report back

Share the results with donors and partners, so you can see what your support did.

Why I ride
I cycle to work, and I arrive feeling energised and ready to start my day. I want every pupil to start their school day the same way.
— Nelson Ataga, Founder

Cycling has always been something I love. Now I want it to do more than keep me fit. Each long ride is a chance to tell people about pupils who need books, and to raise the money to buy them. Every kilometre you sponsor goes toward putting a book in a pupil's hands.
